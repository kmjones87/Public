# Metrolog-inspired sliding ruler (3D printable)

A printable take on the Craighill Metrolog Ruler, the aluminum ruler whose
telescoping neck turns it into a caliper.

## Files

| File | Size (X × Y × Z) |
| --- | --- |
| `Metric Sliding Ruler Body.stl` | 30 × 230 × 5.4 mm |
| `Metric Sliding Ruler Slide.stl` | 30 × 230 × 5.0 mm |

Both parts are 230 mm long. They fit straight on a 256 mm bed; on a
220 × 220 mm bed rotate them about 45° to lie along the diagonal.

## Print settings (from the author)

- Layer height: 0.16 or 0.20 mm
- Infill: 15–20 %, grid or gyroid
- Material: PLA or PETG
- Orientation: flat on the bed
- Add a colour change on the slide so the circle indicator stands out

## Assembly

1. Slide the ruler insert into the body.
2. Push a short piece of 1.75 mm filament into the hole at the top.
3. Trim it flush. It acts as the stop that keeps the slide from coming out.
4. If it works loose, add a drop of super glue or melt the tip.

## Differences from the original Metrolog

| | Craighill Metrolog | This print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Anodized aluminum | PLA / PETG |
| Size | 11" × 1.25" × 0.38" (279 × 32 × 9.7 mm) | 230 × 30 × 5.4 mm |
| Scales | Inches to 8" (1/16") and metric to 20 cm (1 mm) | Metric only |
| Jaw opening | 157 mm | Not stated by the author |

Accuracy depends on printer calibration, so check the printed scale against
a known ruler before relying on it.
